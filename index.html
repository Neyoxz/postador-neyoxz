PostadorNeyoxz

PostadorNeyoxz is a personal video scheduling tool. It lets its owner upload short videos and schedule them for publication on the owner's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts.

How it uses TikTok

  • Login Kit (user.info.basic): the owner signs in with their own TikTok account to authorize the tool.
  • Content Posting API (video.publish): the tool posts videos prepared by the owner to the owner's own account, at the time the owner chose.

The tool runs on the owner's computer. It does not collect data from other users.
