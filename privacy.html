Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. About this tool

PostadorNeyoxz ("the app") is a personal tool used by its owner to schedule and publish the owner's own videos on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. Information we access

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and the basic profile information allowed by the user.info.basic scope. The app uses the video.publish scope only to publish videos chosen by the owner to the owner's own account.

3. How information is stored and used

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are used solely to publish the owner's videos at the scheduled time. The app does not run on any external server and does not send information to any third party other than TikTok, YouTube and Instagram when the owner publishes content.

4. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information with anyone.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens remain on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. Deleting the app's local token folder removes all stored credentials.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
